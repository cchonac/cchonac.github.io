Hi! I'm Yoonhyuck Cho, a Master's student at AIS Lab, Chung-Ang University, advised by Prof. Youn Kyu Lee.

My research focuses on Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), particularly on understanding their visual reasoning and attention behaviors, as well as investigating their vulnerabilities through adversarial attacks.
